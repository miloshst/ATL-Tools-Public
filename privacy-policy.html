Privacy Policy for ATL Tools

Last updated: October 13, 2026

ATL Tools provides aviation calculation tools, including fuel uplift and flight time calculations, that work offline, together with optional online METAR/TAF weather retrieval. Airport search, the bundled airport catalog, Day/Night calculations, airport local-time and Sun calculations, and Help & Guides work offline. Weather retrieval requires an internet connection.

Information Collection and Use

ATL Tools stores appearance preferences, favorite tool selections, saved airport identifiers and cached weather reports in private app storage on your device. No ATL Tools account or GPS/device-location permission is required. The app does not continuously track aircraft or device location.

When you use METAR/TAF, selected airport ICAO identifiers are sent to NOAA Aviation Weather Center at aviationweather.gov over HTTPS. The provider may receive standard connection information, such as the source IP address and the app's User-Agent, as part of these requests. The app does not add a unique user or device identifier to NOAA requests; the User-Agent identifies the app and platform. Providers may log requests. Calculator inputs, crew rosters and personal flight records are not sent to the weather provider.

Values entered into the application's calculators are processed locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Local Data and Retention

Preferences, favorite tools, saved airports and cached weather reports are stored locally on your device. Removing an airport removes it from the saved list, but does not necessarily delete all previously cached report files. Clearing app storage also resets saved selections and preferences. You can remove the app's local data by clearing its storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Cached weather reports are the last available reports and can become stale. They are not a guarantee of current observations or current conditions; check the observation time and any cached or stale indicators.

Accounts

ATL Tools does not require an account. Optional crew-rest names and calculator values are handled on device; Google Play services operate under their own terms and account settings.

Analytics and Advertising

ATL Tools has no advertising or integrated app analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. This does not mean that external providers or platform services never process or log request metadata.

Internet Access

Core calculators, airport search, Day/Night calculations and Help & Guides do not require internet access. Optional METAR/TAF requests are sent directly to aviationweather.gov over HTTPS for selected airports when the Weather screen retrieves or refreshes reports. Periodic refresh occurs while the screen is active.

Google Play in-app update checks and accepted update downloads use the Play Store. Google documents processing of device metadata, the application version, and installed modules and asset packs to determine update availability and download size. Google states that this data is encrypted, not transferred to third parties, and deleted following a fixed retention period; its documentation does not specify that period. See Google Play In-App Updates data safety information and Google Privacy Policy.

AndroidX EmojiCompat automatically requests an emoji font shortly after app startup from a compatible system font provider, often Google Play services. The provider may download the font, and Google documents that font requests are logged. The app requests the font, not your calculator inputs or crew names. Provider-side metadata, retention and network endpoints depend on the installed provider. See Android EmojiCompat documentation.

Update checks and font-provider requests can occur without opening Weather. Core calculations and bundled guides still work offline. NOTAM and Weather Radar are unavailable and remain COMING SOON; neither has an active network service.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell user data and does not receive calculator inputs, crew roster data or personal flight records. Optional weather retrieval transmits selected airport ICAO identifiers and standard HTTPS connection information to NOAA Aviation Weather Center as described above. Platform-provider metadata processing is described above. NOAA and platform providers control their own processing and logs; ATL Tools does not promise a provider retention period or deletion of provider-side records. See the NOAA Aviation Weather Center API documentation and National Weather Service privacy information.

Children's Privacy

ATL Tools does not require users, including children, to create an account or provide personal details to use its core calculators. Optional crew-rest names remain local. Local processing, optional Weather and platform-provider requests operate as described in this policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of ATL Tools changes. Any changes will be published on this page.
